# Desktop app and delivery

This module's code. Its plan is `Preparation/Architecture/Desktop app and delivery.md`, and its row file sits beside it under `Rows/`.

It is built at steps 2 and 11 of `Preparation/Build/Plan.md`. **Step 1 is here already**, because step 1 is the shell itself and the shell is this module's: the window, its own title bar and three buttons, the light-or-dark reading, the two faces, and the typography point.

- `src/main.rs` — the application, and the one command the window's chrome asks for its prose.
- `src/typography.rs` — the typography point and the verbatim route beside it, section 17's X1, X2, X7, X8 and X9.
- `web/` — the page: `index.html`, `smoozy.css`, `smoozy.js`. No inline script and no inline style, per H33.
- `tauri.conf.json` — the shell's own settings: the frame given up, the content-security policy, and the Windows web-runtime value the Creator's ruling of 2026-08-23 names.
- `capabilities/main.json` — what the window may ask the shell for, and nothing beneath it.
- `icons/` — Smoozy's icon for all three systems. **`app-icon.png` is the master**, supplied by the Creator on 2026-09-21 and drawn to Apple's icon grid. It is the Creator's own work and is under the project's licence, GPL-3.0-or-later (Creator-stated, 2026-10-27); every other file here is made from it with the shell's own icon command and none is edited by hand. *(Until 2026-09-21 this line was a pending marker over the placeholder square the shell measurement used.)*
