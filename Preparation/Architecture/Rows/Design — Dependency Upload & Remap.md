**Answer, 2026-08-21: the step is taken, at `Preparation/Architecture/Dependency Upload & Remap.md` [M16a](../Dependency%20Upload%20&%20Remap.md#65-the-two-copies-of-a-linked-blend--row-175).** **A retry is M14's production run once more for that one file** — no check re-run, nothing re-sent — because a *could not be fixed* row is a fixed copy that could not be produced and A11 is written for transfers.
