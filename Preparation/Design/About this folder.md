# Design

This folder holds the **Design** documents — the app's look and feel.

What the Design stage is, and where it sits in the order, is set out in **`../Groundwork/Start Here.md`**. How the stage is actually worked is **[Method](Method.md)**, and where it currently stands is **`Audits/Design/Progress.md`**.

Where a Design document needs to know what Blender actually does, start at **[the map](../Blender%20Research/Across%20topics/The%20map.md)** and go where it sends you. The [Blender Research method](../Blender%20Research/Method.md) says what may be opened from there and what may not.
